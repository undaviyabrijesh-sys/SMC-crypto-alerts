## Delta funding and execution-cost semantics audit — 2026-11-12

- Official BTCUSD product metadata was checked at the public Delta India product endpoint on 2026-11-12: `rate_exchange_interval=28800` seconds (8h), `expiry_interval=28800`, taker commission `0.0005` (5bp/side), maker commission `0.0002` (2bp/side), contract value `0.001 BTC`, tick size `0.5 USD`, funding method `mark_price`. These are current public product defaults, not confirmation of the user's account/VIP fee tier.
- Delta's current perpetual guide states funding is exchanged at 05:30, 13:30 and 21:30 IST (00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC), with a position snapshot at each event; a position must be in the snapshot to pay/receive. Positive funding means longs pay shorts; negative means shorts pay longs. The guide gives the payment as current position value multiplied by the funding rate and illustrates a rate of 0.01% per 8h. It also warns some contracts may use 4/2/1h intervals, so do not assume BTC's interval applies to ETH/XAUT without checking their product metadata.
- Independent inspection of the existing verified derivatives artifact [run 41955898516](https://github.com/undaviyabrijesh-sys/SMC-crypto-alerts/actions/runs/41955898516), artifact 13383865992, found BTC/ETH 1h FUNDING candle values are held flat across hourly rows and, after 2025-09-08, all observed value-change timestamps align to 00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC: BTC 550 change points across 10,119 rows; ETH 641 across 10,119 rows. Delta's guide explicitly says the current 8-hour method became effective at 5:30pm IST on 2025-09-08; before then, funding was exchanged minute-by-minute. Therefore pre-2025-09-08 funding is a different methodology regime and must not be mixed into the current 8-hour event model. The observed post-change cadence matches the documented schedule, but historical candle timestamp-to-realization mapping still needs an event-level cross-check; do not apply it to P&L yet.
- For the same artifact, raw funding closes include negative values and have median 0.01 (raw API units). The guide's percentage convention makes 0.01 consistent with 0.01%, but this remains a provisional historical-series interpretation pending event-level cross-check. Never sum the repeated hourly rows as hourly cashflows; the repeated 8h rate would be double-counted.
- Cost-model interpretation: current BTCUSD default taker/taker round trip is 10bp before slippage; the existing 12bp baseline is therefore interpretable as 10bp taker fees plus an explicit 2bp combined slippage/spread stress. Maker/maker would be 4bp before slippage; limit-entry/market-stop mixes require side-specific fee treatment. Preserve 6/12/20bp stress results and do not rewrite historical backtests as if the user's actual fee tier were known.
- Official references: [BTCUSD product metadata](https://api.india.delta.exchange/v2/products/BTCUSD), [Delta perpetual funding guide](https://guides.delta.exchange/delta-exchange-india-user-guide/derivatives-guide/docs), [Delta historical candles API](https://docs.delta.exchange/).
- Next: inspect ETH and XAUT product metadata separately; cross-check one BTC/ETH historical funding event against a realized settlement record or exchange-provided event history; only then implement event-time funding attribution with explicit 00/08/16 UTC snapshots for BTC and contract-specific schedules for other instruments.
