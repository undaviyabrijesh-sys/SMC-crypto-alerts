## Frozen hypotheses and method

Six independent hypotheses were screened on 15m entries with completed 4h context and 1h entries with completed 1d context:

1. `TREND_PULLBACK`: EMA20/EMA50 trend, recent EMA20 touch and reclaim.
2. `BREAKOUT_RETEST`: close through prior 20-bar extreme with higher-timeframe trend alignment. This first screen tests the breakout trigger, not a later retest.
3. `LIQUIDITY_SWEEP`: wick beyond the prior 20-bar high/low and close back inside.
4. `MEAN_REVERSION`: Bollinger(20,2) re-entry with RSI filter.
5. `VOL_EXPANSION`: ATR14 above 1.5× trailing median ATR100 and 20-bar breakout in higher-timeframe direction.
6. `MOMENTUM`: five-bar return above/below 0.8% in higher-timeframe direction with EMA20 alignment.

Execution/replay assumptions:
- Signal is known only after the signal candle closes; entry is the next contiguous candle open.
- Stop = 1.5 × signal-bar ATR(14); target = 2.0R; max holding period = 96 bars; one position per strategy stream.
- Same-bar stop/target ambiguity resolves to stop-first. An opening gap through a resting target is resolved at the target before considering later intrabar movement; this was fixed in the repository replay engine and covered by long/short regression tests.
- A missing candle cancels a pending entry or closes an open position at the next observed open; no interpolation or forward-fill.
- Chronological 60% train / 20% validation / 20% OOS test, with 20-bar purge and 2-bar embargo at split boundaries. Five sequential expanding-window walk-forward test blocks were also evaluated. Rules were fixed before the split; no parameter fitting or OOS tuning was done.
- Base cost: 5 bps taker fee + 2 bps slippage + 2 bps spread proxy per side. Stress cost: 5 bps fee + 5 bps slippage + 5 bps spread proxy per side. The spread values are explicit stress assumptions, not measured historical spreads.
- Funding was excluded because historical settlement-event attribution/units are not independently verified. Historical bid/ask, depth and market impact are unavailable. This is a price-unit OHLCV simulation, not a Delta contract/margin/liquidation emulator.

## Walk-forward and regime check

- 180 stressed walk-forward folds were evaluated (six hypotheses × three assets × two timeframes × five chronological blocks).
- Only 8/180 folds had both positive expectancy and PF above 1; only 4/180 also had at least 30 trades. The four larger positive folds were isolated: ETH 15m volatility expansion in folds 1–2, ETH 1h breakout in fold 3, and ETH 1h liquidity sweep in fold 5. Their complete OOS test cells remained negative, so no stable cross-fold behaviour was established.
- Some OOS regime subgroups differed (for example, ETH 1h liquidity sweeps were slightly positive in the high-volatility subset but materially negative in low volatility). These are descriptive post-split observations, not validated regime filters; no subgroup was promoted.
